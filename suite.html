How it connects to PraiseAV Windows

The Suite is built for PraiseAV Windows. Its service file carries the plan into the live presentation app.

1. Plan in the Suite

Choose the running order, song keys and arrangements, timings and team.

2. Export the service

Download the .praiseav file, or send it to the Windows Services folder from a browser that supports folder access.

3. Open it on the laptop

Load the service in PraiseAV Windows before Sunday. Export and reopen it after any later Suite changes.

How your church gets access

Church data belongs to the church’s own Firebase project. PraiseAV Windows works on its own if you do not use the Suite.

For the church administrator

Find your church first. If it is new, reserve its name and town, set up a Firebase project on the free Spark plan, connect the Suite, and share the church’s invite link. The administrator reviews access requests and chooses roles.

Church setup guide ↗

For a church member

Open your church’s link and sign in with Google, or create an account using your email address and a password. Email accounts verify their address using the link Firebase sends. No Google account is required.

Once verified, you can add your own private name, contact number and household details. A church administrator must approve access before you can view shared church records. After approval, members can use the church directory. Each adult chooses whether to share their contact details with other church members.

Using the Suite today

Shared church mode uses Firestore in the church’s Firebase project and is available on the free Spark plan within Firebase’s usage limits. Open the Suite to find your church or sign in through your church’s invitation link. A new church follows the one-time setup to connect its own Firebase project. Creating that project currently requires the church administrator to follow the setup guide.

Church members sign in with Google or a verified email account. Administrators assign roles, families manage their own details, and members choose which contact details to share. Worship leaders send the running order and song keys to the band; preachers prepare notes, slides, video and music for Sunday. On church Wi-Fi, approved tablets follow the congregation slides or open preacher controls through PraiseAV Windows.
