Free presentation software for churches

Songs, Bible readings, videos and sermons on your screens, simple enough for any volunteer to run on a Sunday morning.

Coming soon for Windows

What it does

Songs and the Bible

A song library you can search by any words. Bible readings as you type a reference, and the NIV and other translations by copying from bible.com.

Coming from EasyWorship?

Bring your songs, schedules and backgrounds across. OpenSong and SongSelect files too.

Videos, PowerPoint and PDF

Moving backgrounds, videos, music, and PowerPoint or PDF slides, without needing PowerPoint. Or design your own slides.

Screens for everyone

Words for the congregation, notes and a timer for the preacher, and a words strip for the livestream, each on its own screen.

Livestream ready

Works with ATEM video switchers. Show another church’s live stream with your own words and logo.

Parent alerts and notices

Crèche and Sunday school alerts with your own group names, scrolling notices and announcement loops.

Phone remote

Run it from a phone or tablet on your church Wi-Fi, with nothing to install. A preacher’s remote for their own slides and notes.

Plan at home, run at church

Save services to OneDrive, Google Drive or a USB stick. Print service sheets and the song report for your CCLI licence.

PraiseAV is free, with no subscription, no account and no adverts, made independently in the UK for churches of every size.
