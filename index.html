Every picture here is PraiseAV itself.

Songs and the Bible, in seconds

  • Find any song by its title, a line of the words or the author.
  • SongSelect without leaving PraiseAV: search it with your own SongSelect account, click Download, and the song is added to your library.
  • Bring your songs with you from EasyWorship, OpenSong, OpenLyrics and SongSelect files (words and chord charts), chord sheet PDFs from a band’s website, or a whole song book. Or paste the words.
  • Bible readings as you type a reference, with the BSB and WEB built in. The NIV, KJV and others by copying from bible.com.
  • Chords for the band (paste a chord sheet and the chords go over the right words), song order, backing tracks, and videos that sing along with the words.
Songs · The Bible

A screen for everyone

  • The congregation: words, verses, videos and pictures, on a projector or TVs.
  • The preacher and band: the clock, a sermon countdown, the next slide, private notes, chords, and quiet prompts like “5 minutes left”.
  • Practice screens on one laptop at home, to set up the whole service before Sunday.
  • Screens on the network: the words in OBS or vMix, or the service on a TV in another room, with no cables.
Screens · Sermons

Cameras, a livestream and a recording

  • Go live on YouTube (or Facebook) with your stream key: the camera, the words and the sound desk’s sound, in up to 1080p.
  • Record the service to the laptop, then copy it to a USB stick, checked before it says it’s safe to take out.
  • Old phones as cameras over the church Wi-Fi, with nothing to install, plus webcams and capture devices. Click a camera to cut to it.
  • Sound and picture kept in step, and a sound meter so you know the desk’s sound is coming in.
  • Already have an ATEM Mini? PraiseAV works with it too.
Going live · Recording · Live video

Run it from a phone, or with no volunteer at all

  • Phone remote: Next, Back, Black and the whole service on any phone or tablet on the church Wi-Fi. No app to install.
  • A preacher’s remote for their own slides and notes.
  • No one free at the laptop? The worship leader runs the words and the preacher runs the sermon, each from their phone.
  • Clickers, MIDI foot pedals and Stream Deck buttons work too.

Music, videos and slides

  • Spotify in the top bar: start your pre-service playlist in Spotify, then pause it, skip a song, and fade it out as the service starts, without leaving PraiseAV.
  • Videos, pictures and moving backgrounds, with free starter backgrounds included.

  • Notices that loop before the service, and a countdown to the start.

Why I created PraiseAV

Built for churches. Rooted in faith. Shaped by a DIY attitude.

PraiseAV wasn’t born in a software company’s boardroom. It started with a simple idea: churches shouldn’t have to spend a fortune on software just to put song lyrics, Bible passages and other content on a screen.

Having been involved in church life and audio-visual technology, I know how much work goes on behind the scenes every Sunday. Much of it is done by volunteers, often giving up their own time to make sure everything runs smoothly.

Good presentation software should make their lives easier, not add another complication or another bill to the church budget.

That’s why I started developing PraiseAV.

I grew up with the DIY mentality that runs through punk and alternative music. The idea that you don’t need to wait for somebody else to make something happen. If you’ve got the skills, the tools and the determination, you get stuck in and give it a go.

I’m developing PraiseAV independently, not as a commercial venture, but because that DIY spirit has always been part of who I am. If I can build something that helps other churches, takes a bit of pressure off their AV teams and makes life easier for the volunteers working behind the scenes, then that’s a job worth doing.

The connection to Waken The Dead

If you’ve come across my other project, Waken The Dead, you might be wondering what Christian punk, metal and alternative music have to do with church presentation software.

On the surface, not very much!

Waken The Dead began back in 2000 as a photocopied music zine, driven by my love of alternative music and my Christian faith. It eventually grew into a website dedicated to discovering and sharing Christian music that didn’t always fit into the mainstream.

It was DIY from the beginning. No big company behind it, just a passion for the music, the message and a willingness to put the work in.

PraiseAV comes from exactly that same mentality.

They’re different projects, but they’re both rooted in my Christian faith, a love of creating things and a belief that what we make should have a purpose beyond ourselves.

Waken The Dead is about helping people discover music and connecting with a community. PraiseAV is about giving churches the tools to share music, Scripture and the Gospel without unnecessary expense or complication.

And while Waken The Dead has its roots firmly in the alternative Christian scene, PraiseAV is for everyone. Traditional churches, contemporary churches, small fellowships and larger congregations.

More than just software

I’m not expecting PraiseAV to change the world.

But if it helps a small church working with a tight budget, makes preparing a service a little easier, or means the person running the projector can spend less time fighting with software and more time being part of the service, I’ll consider that a success.

Ultimately, the technology isn’t the important part.

It’s the people, the worship and the message that matter.

PraiseAV is simply something I can contribute, built with the same DIY spirit that’s been with me all these years.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no paid version, no account and no adverts. PraiseAV is made by an independent developer in the UK so that churches on tight budgets can do everything bigger churches do. If you’d like to help, donations are welcome: see why I created PraiseAV.

When can we get it?

PraiseAV is coming to the Microsoft Store for Windows. Get in touch and we’ll let you know as soon as it’s ready, or help you try it at your church sooner.

Do we need the internet?

No. Songs, the Bible, videos, recording and all the screens work without it. You only need the internet to livestream, to show a YouTube or Vimeo link, to download songs from SongSelect, or to copy the NIV from bible.com.

What about song licences?

You still need your CCLI licence to show song words, and a CCLI Streaming Licence (or your country’s equivalent) to livestream them. PraiseAV keeps track of every song you use and makes the report for you.

Which Bibles come with it?

The Berean Standard Bible (BSB) and the World English Bible (WEB) are built in, and work without the internet. The NIV, the King James Version and many others can be shown by copying the verses from bible.com, and PraiseAV turns them into slides.

Do we still need an ATEM or OBS to livestream?

No. PraiseAV can go live on YouTube or Facebook by itself, and record at the same time. If you already have an ATEM Mini or use OBS, PraiseAV works with those too.

Does it work on a Mac?

Not at the moment: PraiseAV is for Windows. An old Windows laptop is usually enough.

What about our privacy?

PraiseAV has no account and no tracking. Your songs, services and recordings stay on your computer. See the privacy policy.
