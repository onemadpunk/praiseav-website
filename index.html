What it does

Every picture here is PraiseAV itself.

Songs and the Bible, in seconds

  • Find any song by its title, a line of the words or the author.
  • Bring your songs with you from EasyWorship, OpenSong, OpenLyrics and SongSelect (words and chord charts), or paste the words.
  • Bible readings as you type a reference, with the BSB, KJV and WEB built in. The NIV and others by copying from bible.com.
  • Chords for the band, song order, backing tracks, and videos that sing along with the words.
Songs · The Bible

A screen for everyone

  • The congregation: words, verses, videos and pictures, on a projector or TVs.
  • The preacher and band: the clock, a sermon countdown, the next slide, private notes, chords, and quiet prompts like “5 minutes left”.
  • Practice screens on one laptop at home, to set up the whole service before Sunday.
  • Screens on the network: the words in OBS or vMix, or the service on a TV in another room, with no cables.
Screens · Sermons

Cameras, a livestream and a recording

  • Go live on YouTube (or Facebook) with your stream key: the camera, the words and the sound desk’s sound, in up to 1080p.
  • Record the service to the laptop, then copy it to a USB stick, checked before it says it’s safe to take out.
  • Old phones as cameras over the church Wi-Fi, with nothing to install, plus webcams and capture devices. Click a camera to cut to it.
  • Sound and picture kept in step, and a sound meter so you know the desk’s sound is coming in.
  • Already have an ATEM Mini? PraiseAV works with it too.
Going live · Recording · Live video

Run it from a phone, or with no volunteer at all

  • Phone remote: Next, Back, Black and the whole service on any phone or tablet on the church Wi-Fi. No app to install.
  • A preacher’s remote for their own slides and notes.
  • No one free at the laptop? The worship leader runs the words and the preacher runs the sermon, each from their phone.
  • Clickers, MIDI foot pedals and Stream Deck buttons work too.

Why PraiseAV exists

A church plant, a PowerPoint, and a bit of punk.

A while ago I visited a church plant. There weren’t many people there, and the song words were up on the screen in a basic PowerPoint. They were doing their best with what they had.

It stayed with me. Most church AV teams take certain things for granted: words that keep up with the band, notes and a countdown for the preacher, a camera and a livestream for the people who can’t be there. But nearly all of it comes with a subscription, and for a small church or a new plant, every pound counts. I thought there should be free software that lets any church do what’s normal for a church AV team, without the money worry.

I also run Waken the Dead, a site for Christian punk, metal and alternative music. To me, a big part of punk is simple: if you see something that needs doing, don’t wait for someone else to do it. Do it yourself.

So I did. That’s where PraiseAV comes from.

It’s free, and it takes some of the pressure off you having to do it all yourself too, so your team can get on with Sunday.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no paid version, no account and no adverts. PraiseAV is made by an independent developer in the UK so that churches on tight budgets can do everything bigger churches do. If you’d like to help, donations are welcome: see why PraiseAV exists.

When can we get it?

PraiseAV is coming to the Microsoft Store for Windows. Book a demo and we’ll let you know as soon as it’s ready, or help you try it at your church sooner.

Do we need the internet?

No. Songs, the Bible, videos, recording and all the screens work without it. You only need the internet to livestream, to show a YouTube link, or to copy the NIV from bible.com.

What about song licences?

You still need your CCLI licence to show song words, and a CCLI Streaming Licence (or your country’s equivalent) to livestream them. PraiseAV keeps track of every song you use and makes the report for you.

Which Bibles come with it?

The Berean Standard Bible (BSB), the King James Version (KJV) and the World English Bible (WEB) are built in. The NIV and many others can be shown by copying the verses from bible.com, and PraiseAV turns them into slides.

Do we still need an ATEM or OBS to livestream?

No. PraiseAV can go live on YouTube or Facebook by itself, and record at the same time. If you already have an ATEM Mini or use OBS, PraiseAV works with those too.

Does it work on a Mac?

Not at the moment: PraiseAV is for Windows. An old Windows laptop is usually enough.

What about our privacy?

PraiseAV has no account and no tracking. Your songs, services and recordings stay on your computer. See the privacy policy.
