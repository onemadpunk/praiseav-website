    Building a service

    The Bible

    The Bible tab finds readings as you type, and puts them on screen with the reference and translation on every slide.

    Find a reading

    Type the reference into the box. Short forms are fine:

    You type You get
    jn 3 16 John 3:16
    john 3:16-18 John 3:16–18
    ps 23 Psalm 23 (the whole psalm)
    1 cor 13 4-7 1 Corinthians 13:4–7
    rom 8:28-9:1 Romans 8:28 to 9:1

    Or type a few words (“good shepherd”) to find verses that contain them. Click a result to open it.

    Then:

    • Enter or Add to service adds the reading to the service.
    • Shift+Enter or Show now adds it and puts it on screen straight away.

    Translations

    PraiseAV includes three translations, which work without the internet:

    • BSB: the Berean Standard Bible, modern English (the first choice in English)
    • WEB: the World English Bible
    • RV1909: the Reina-Valera 1909, in Spanish (the first choice when PraiseAV is in Spanish)

    The Bibles in the language PraiseAV is set to come first in the lists, and the first of them is used until you choose another. The others are still there, after them. A Spanish Bible's references are in Spanish: Juan 3:16.

    For the King James Version (KJV), use bible.com, the same way as the NIV (below). In the UK the KJV belongs to the Crown, so PraiseAV can’t include the whole of it without permission.

    Choose the translation in the box next to the search. A reading already in the service can be switched to another translation with the box above its slides.

    The NIV and other translations

    The NIV, ESV, NLT and others can’t be included in PraiseAV, but you can show the verses your service uses:

    1. Type the reference, then click NIV and more from bible.com. bible.com opens in its own window, at that chapter.
    2. Tap the verses you want (they’re underlined), then press bible.com’s Copy.
    3. The verses appear in PraiseAV’s Bible tab. Click Add to service or Show now.

    You can also copy verses from Bible Gateway or anywhere else, and click Paste verses (or paste into the search box). Include the reference if it’s not from bible.com.

    • Copied verses are kept with the service, so they work at church without the internet.
    • Every slide shows the translation after the reference, like “(NIV)”. That’s what the NIV’s publisher asks for on church slides.

    Note: Churches can quote up to 500 verses of the NIV without asking permission, with the translation named. A Sunday’s readings are well within that.

    Bible settings

    Open Bible settings at the bottom of the Bible tab:

    • How much of a reading goes on each slide: As much as fits (the default), Shorter slides, or One verse per slide.
    • Translations to show in the lists: untick the ones your church doesn’t use. (The translation in use can’t be hidden.)

    How readings look

    Readings use the theme for Bible verses. Many churches choose High contrast (easy to read) for readings. See Themes.
