    Setups and hardware

    Example setups

    Every church is different, but most fit one of these. Start with the simplest that does what you need. You can always add to it later, and practice screens let you try a bigger setup before buying anything.

    For what to buy, see Hardware.

    Tip: Count the screens that show different things. Each one needs its own output from the computer. Two screens showing the same thing can share one output with a splitter.

    1. One laptop and a projector

    The simplest setup, and where most churches start.

    You need: a laptop with an HDMI socket, an HDMI cable long enough to reach, and the projector or TV.

    In PraiseAV: nothing to set up. The projector gets the congregation view by itself.

    Good for: small churches, house churches, halls, a first try.

    The two front projectors show the same thing, so a splitter feeds both from one output. Windows sees them as one screen.

    You need: a desktop PC with a graphics card with three or four outputs, an HDMI splitter (1 in, 2 out), and extenders for the long runs.

    In PraiseAV: in Screens…, give each output its job: Congregation, Preacher, Timer.

    Careful: Both projectors on a splitter must handle the same resolution. Set them both to 1920 × 1080.

    You need: an ATEM Mini Pro, one or two cameras with HDMI out, HDMI cables, an Ethernet cable from the ATEM to the router, and an audio cable from the sound desk.

    In PraiseAV:

    1. In Screens…, give the ATEM output the Livestream job, with a Black background.
    2. Click ATEM…, connect, choose Downstream key 1, and tick Turn the words on and off by themselves.
    3. On Sunday, press ● Go live at the top of PraiseAV.

    Full steps: Livestream and ATEM.

    Important: The stream needs its own sound mix from the sound desk (an aux send). The sound in the room is mostly from the speakers, so a room microphone sounds hollow and echoey online. Ask whoever runs sound to set up an aux with every microphone and instrument.

    8. Setting up at home

    The AV volunteer can set everything up at home, with no church equipment.

    LaptopPraiseAV with practice screens
    HDMI
    Spare TV or monitorpretend projector
    1. In Screens…, click Open practice screens to see every view side by side.
    2. Make the themes, alert groups and logo, and plan which screen does what.
    3. Save a Church setup to OneDrive or a USB stick, and open it on the church computer. See Church setup.

    This is also how to show a church what PraiseAV can do before they’ve bought any screens.

    Where to put the computer

    • At the back, where the operator can see the screens and the front, and hear clearly.
    • With a clicker or the phone remote, the operator can sit anywhere.
    • Keep cables taped down or under mats where people walk.
    • Plug the computer into the same power as the projector if you can, or use a small UPS (battery backup) so a power blip doesn’t stop the service.
