    Building a service

    PraiseAV Suite: planning together

    PraiseAV Suite is a free website for planning with your team: services, songs and keys, rotas, sermons, people, check-ins, groups, rooms and giving records. PraiseAV on the church computer then runs the service. You don’t need the Suite to use PraiseAV.

    Open it at praiseav-suite.web.app, or from your church’s invitation link. It works on a computer, tablet or phone.

    Setting up your church

    One person, the church administrator, does this once. They need a Google account that the church controls. Everyone else just gets a link.

    1. Open the Suite, type your church’s name and town and click Find church. If your church is already there, ask its administrator for the church link instead.
    2. If it isn’t there, click Set up this church and sign in with the church’s Google account. This holds the name for about two weeks while you finish.
    3. Follow the Church setup checklist. It links straight to each page you need in Firebase (Google’s free service that keeps your church’s records): make a project on the free Spark plan, add a web app and paste its code into the Suite, switch on Google and Email/Password sign-in, and create the database.
    4. Click Copy security rules, paste them into Firebase’s Firestore Rules page, and publish.
    5. Tick I published these rules, then click Connect and sign in.

    Your church’s records live in your church’s own Firebase project, not with PraiseAV. Free use has limits; a typical church stays well inside them.

    When the Suite says your rules are out of date (after a Suite update), open Church access, copy the updated rules, and publish them in Firebase the same way.

    Inviting the team

    Open Church access and copy the church link, or type someone’s email to open an invitation in your own email app. People sign in with Google, or create an account with any email address and a password (they click the link in the email Firebase sends to confirm it). Nobody sees church records until an administrator approves them and gives them a role.

    Every church uses the same Suite website; the church link takes people to your own church. Put it on your church’s website as a “Church login” button if you like.

    Let members find you by name: in Church access, the person who set the church up can click List the church. Then anyone can open the Suite, type the church’s name and town, click Find church and Sign in to your church, with no link needed. They still need your approval. Stop listing the church turns it off.

    Forgotten password? Reset password on the sign-in page sends a reset email.

    Connecting the church computer

    Do this once, so services arrive in PraiseAV by themselves.

    1. In the Suite, open Church access → Set up the booth computer and click Copy booth code.
    2. On the church computer, open PraiseAV → More → Church sharing, paste the code under PraiseAV Suite, type an email and password for the booth, and click Create the booth login.
    3. Click the link in the email that arrives, then click Continue in PraiseAV.
    4. Back in the Suite, a request marked Booth computer appears. Click Approve as booth computer.

    Use a church email for the booth, such as yourchurch.avdesk@gmail.com. The booth login only sees services and songs, never people, giving or children’s records. PraiseAV checks for new things about once a minute while it’s open.

    Planning a service and sending it

    1. In Services & Order, click New plan, then Add item for songs, readings, the sermon, prayers and notices. Choose each song’s key for this service.
    2. Open PraiseAV export and click Send to the booth. The Suite shows when the church computer has the latest version.
    3. Change something later? Send it again. If the service is already open in PraiseAV, the AV desk sees Load update and chooses a safe moment. Nothing on screen changes by surprise.

    A guest speaker’s PowerPoint or PDF can be added to its row from any device, even an iPad: it travels through the church cloud and PraiseAV turns it into slides. No booth connected? Send to PraiseAV folder (Chrome or Edge, with a shared OneDrive or Google Drive folder) or Download .praiseav still work. See Services.

    Old services: Export all services downloads a spreadsheet of every service (each item, key, leader and CCLI number) to keep. Delete service removes the plan you’re looking at, and Delete song in the song library removes a song. Both ask first, and can’t be undone.

    Sermons from home

    The planner puts the sermon in the service and gives the preacher’s email in My sermon & tablet. The preacher signs in, writes private notes and congregation slides (use --- between pages), adds videos, music, PowerPoint or PDF, and clicks Send my sermon to church. Videos up to 100 MB and music up to 50 MB go through the church cloud; bigger videos need the shared folder.

    On Sunday the preacher’s tablet joins the church Wi-Fi and is approved as a preacher remote. See Phone and tablet remote.

    Letting the band know

    When the songs and keys are set, the worship leader clicks Let the team know on the service. Everyone on the rota sees You’re on the rota in Band stand and answers I can serve or Can’t make it; their answers show beside the rota. It also opens the leader’s own email app with the team in Bcc and the order filled in, so one press of Send emails them too. Tell the team again repeats it after a change.

    Musicians can open Band stand at home to read the charts in their own key and capo, or print a songbook. At church, their tablets follow the live slides through PraiseAV’s band stand.

    Who can see what

    Role Can see Can change
    Owner, admin Everything Everything
    Planner, worship leader Services and songs Services and songs
    Preacher Services, songs and their own sermon drafts Their own sermons
    AV, musician Services and songs Nothing
    People The people list, including pastoral notes The people list
    Kids Check-ins, pickup codes and medical notes Check-ins
    Finance Giving records Giving records
    Member, family Groups, rooms and the opt-in church directory Their own details

    A person can have more than one role. In the Church directory, each adult chooses whether to show their name, household, email and phone; it’s all off until they choose. Giving records are a record for the church’s finance team, not a payment system or a Gift Aid claim. Backups you download contain personal details and aren’t encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.
