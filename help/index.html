    PraiseAV Help

    Welcome. PraiseAV puts songs, Bible readings, videos and sermons on your church’s screens. It’s free, and it’s made to be simple enough for any volunteer on a Sunday morning.

    New to PraiseAV?

    Start with Your first Sunday. It takes you from installing PraiseAV to running a whole service, one step at a time. No experience needed.

    Used EasyWorship before? Coming from EasyWorship shows where everything is, and how to bring your songs and backgrounds across.
