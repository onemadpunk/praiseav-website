    Screens and livestream

    Going live on YouTube

    PraiseAV can livestream the service itself: the congregation screen (the camera with the words, or the slides) and the sound desk's sound, straight to YouTube. No ATEM, and no other program to learn.

    Once: the stream key

    1. In YouTube Studio, click Create › Go live, then Stream.
      • The very first time, YouTube may ask you to confirm your channel, and it can take up to a day before live streaming is switched on.
    2. Copy the Stream key (click Copy beside it).
    3. In PraiseAV, click ◉ Go live at the top, paste the key into Stream key, and click Save.

    The key is kept on this computer, protected by Windows, and is only ever sent to YouTube. Keep it private: anyone with it can stream to your channel. If it ever gets out, click Reset beside it in YouTube Studio and paste the new one into PraiseAV.

    Tip: In YouTube Studio's stream settings, switch on Auto-start and Auto-stop. Then the broadcast starts by itself when PraiseAV goes live, and ends when it stops, with no need to touch YouTube on Sunday.

    On Sunday

    1. Click ◉ Go live.
    2. Check Sound from is the sound desk's input, and the meter moves when someone speaks or sings.
    3. Choose the picture quality to suit the church's internet:
      • Good: 720p for most churches (about 4 Mbps upload).
      • Best: 1080p if the internet is fast (about 8 Mbps upload).
      • Slow internet: 480p if the stream keeps saying the internet is slow.
    4. Leave Also save a recording on this computer ticked, for a copy to keep (and to upload again if the stream had trouble).
    5. Click ● Go live now.

    The button at the top turns red: ● LIVE and how long you've been live. Run the service as normal: whatever's on the congregation screen goes out, with the camera and words if Live video is on, or the slides if not.

    To finish, click ● LIVE and say OK. If Also save a recording was ticked, the recording's message has Copy to USB stick.

    If the internet struggles

    • · slow internet on the button: the internet can't keep up, so a few pictures are being skipped to stay up to date. If it happens a lot, use a lower picture quality next time, or plug the computer into the router with a network cable (much better than Wi-Fi).
    • · reconnecting…: the connection dropped. PraiseAV keeps trying by itself and carries on the same broadcast when it's back. You don't need to do anything.

    Sound and picture in step

    The sound is held back to match whichever camera is on screen, as for recordings. Measure each camera once: see Sound and picture in step.

    Facebook and others

    In Stream to, choose Facebook and paste the stream key from Facebook's Live Producer. For another service, choose Another service and paste its RTMP address (it starts with rtmp:// or rtmps://) and its stream key.

    Licences: livestreaming songs needs a CCLI Streaming Licence (or your country's equivalent), and permission for any videos you show.

    With an ATEM, or OBS

    You don't need them for this, but PraiseAV still works with both: see Livestream and ATEM.
