    Looking after PraiseAV

    Getting help

    PraiseAV is free, and looked after by a very small team. Here’s the quickest way to get an answer.

    Look it up first

    • Type a few words in Search the help at the top of this page.
    • Troubleshooting covers the common problems: screens, sound, video, the internet and the projector.
    • The questions at the bottom of praiseav.com cover cost, licences, languages and privacy.

    Report a problem from PraiseAV

    Something not working? Click Report a problem… at the bottom right of PraiseAV. Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened, then click Email it…. The report adds the Windows and PraiseAV versions and recent errors, but never your songs, services, names or stream key. You can read every word before you send it. See Troubleshooting.

    Email us

    Questions, ideas and problems are all welcome at onemadpunk+praiseav@gmail.com. It helps to say:

    • your church’s name, and whether it’s about PraiseAV on Windows or the Suite,
    • what you were trying to do, and what happened instead,
    • a photo or screenshot of the screen, if you can.

    We answer as soon as we can, but it can take a few days. Don’t leave it until Sunday morning: try things out midweek, and keep a backup.

    Questions about the Suite

    Each church’s Suite runs in the church’s own Firebase project, so PraiseAV can’t see your records, sign anyone in, or bring back deleted records.

    • Can’t sign in, or need access? Ask your church’s administrator. They approve people and give them roles. A forgotten password can be reset with Reset password on the sign-in page.
    • Something deleted? Your church administrator can bring it back from the church’s backup. See Backing up your church.
    • Anything else: email us as above.

    Song licences

    PraiseAV doesn’t sell or give out song licences. See Song licences, and ask CCLI about your licence.
