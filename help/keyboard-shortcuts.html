    Keyboard shortcuts

    You can run a whole service from the keyboard, or with a presentation clicker.

    Running a service

    Key What it does
    → ↓ Space Page Down Next slide (at the end of an item, the first slide of the next one)
    ← ↑ Page Up Previous slide
    B or . Black screen (press again to come back)
    C Clear text: hide the words, keep the background
    L Logo: show your church logo
    V Live video on or off
    [ and ] The preacher’s notes: previous and next page

    Tip: Presentation clickers send Page Down, Page Up and usually B or . for their blank-screen button, so they work with PraiseAV without any setting up.

    Everywhere else

    Key What it does
    F1 Help about what you’re doing
    Ctrl+S Save the service
    Esc Close a window or dialog
    Enter (in the Bible tab) Add the reading to the service
    Shift+Enter (in the Bible tab) Add the reading and show it now

    In the slide designer

    Key What it does
    Arrow keys Nudge the selected item (hold Shift to move one pixel)
    Delete Remove the selected item
    Ctrl+D Copy the selected item
    Ctrl+Z Undo
    Esc Deselect (press again to close)

    The keys don’t do anything while you’re typing in a box, so you can’t change the screen by accident while searching for a song.
