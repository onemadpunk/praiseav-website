    Building a service

    Song licences: CCLI and SongSelect

    PraiseAV is free and independent. It isn’t part of CCLI or SongSelect, and it doesn’t come with copyrighted song words or chords. The songs you add are your church’s own, so your church needs the right licences for them, just as it would with any other worship software.

    This page is a plain guide, not legal advice. Licences differ by country and change over time, so check the details with CCLI or your country’s licensing body.

    Showing and printing song words

    Most modern worship songs are under copyright. To show their words on a screen or print them on a service sheet, churches usually need a CCLI Church Copyright Licence (or the equivalent where you live). CCLI then asks you to report which songs you use, and PraiseAV keeps count for you: see CCLI report.

    Old hymns are often in the public domain. Be careful with newer versions and arrangements of them, which can be under copyright.

    Livestreaming and recordings

    Showing song words in a livestream, or putting a recorded service online, usually needs a CCLI Streaming Licence (or your country’s equivalent) as well as the Church Copyright Licence. PraiseAV counts the songs shown while you’re streaming, for that report too.

    Playing a recording (a backing track or a music video) in a stream may need permission of its own. If you’re not sure, ask CCLI or the song’s publisher.

    SongSelect

    SongSelect is CCLI’s song library: a separate subscription that gives you song words and chord sheets. If your church has an account, click From SongSelect in the Songs tab, sign in with the church’s own SongSelect account, and download songs straight into PraiseAV. You type the password into SongSelect’s own page, so PraiseAV never sees it. See Songs.

    SongSelect’s own terms apply to what you download. Use songs your church is licensed for, and keep them for your church’s use.

    Chord charts for the band

    The band screen, the Band stand in PraiseAV Suite and printed chord sheets show the chords your church has added. Before sharing chord charts with musicians, check that your licence or SongSelect plan covers it.

    What PraiseAV does and doesn’t do

    • It counts the songs you show, print and stream, and makes a report you can copy into CCLI’s reporting.
    • It doesn’t send anything to CCLI, and it doesn’t check whether a song is covered by your licence.
    • It doesn’t supply copyrighted songs. The hymns that come with PraiseAV are old, public domain words.
