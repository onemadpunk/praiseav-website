    Looking after PraiseAV

    Troubleshooting

    Important: In the middle of a service, press Black (B). It always works, and gives you a moment to sort things out.

    The projector shows nothing

    1. Is Black or Logo on at the top? Press it again.
    2. Is the projector on, and on the right input (HDMI 1, HDMI 2…)? Use its remote.
    3. Press Windows+P and choose Extend.
    4. In PraiseAV, click Hide then Show at the top right.
    5. Open Screens…. Is the projector listed, with the Congregation job?
    6. Unplug the HDMI cable at both ends and plug it back in.

    The projector shows my desktop, or a copy of my screen

    Windows is copying (duplicating) your screen. Press Windows+P and choose Extend. PraiseAV then finds the projector.

    The words are on the wrong screen

    Open Screens…. On the screen map, tap the two screens to swap them. PraiseAV remembers this for next time.

    A video won’t play

    PraiseAV says if a file won’t play. Some formats (WMV, AVI, ProRes) can’t be read. Convert the video to MP4 with the free HandBrake app (choose the “Fast 1080p30” preset) and add it again.

    If a video stutters, it may be 4K or very large. A 1080p MP4 plays smoothly on almost any computer.

    No sound

    • Click the speaker icon by the clock (bottom right of Windows) and check the right output is chosen and not muted.
    • Check the Volume under Music and audio.
    • Check the sound desk channel is up.
    • Videos play their sound from the congregation screen’s window; music from Music and audio. Both use the Windows output.

    The words are too small or too big

    Go to the Themes tab, click the theme, and change Largest text size. See Themes. Long verses shrink by themselves to fit.

    A PowerPoint won’t come in

    PraiseAV needs Microsoft PowerPoint or the free LibreOffice to open PowerPoint files. Install LibreOffice, or ask for a PDF. See PowerPoint and PDF.

    The camera isn’t in the list

    • Click Refresh in Live video.
    • Close any other program using the camera (Zoom, Teams, OBS, the Camera app).
    • Plug the capture device into a blue USB 3 socket, directly, not through a hub.
    • See Live video.

    A phone can’t connect to the remote

    It must be on the same Wi-Fi as the PraiseAV computer (not guest Wi-Fi), and Windows must allow PraiseAV on private networks. See Phone remote.

    The ATEM won’t connect

    • The ATEM must be plugged into the network with an Ethernet cable, the same network as the computer.
    • Check its address in Blackmagic’s ATEM Setup app (with the ATEM plugged in by USB).
    • A new ATEM Mini uses 192.168.10.240. If your router gives addresses like 192.168.1.x, change the ATEM’s address in ATEM Setup to one in that range (for example 192.168.1.240).
    • See Livestream and ATEM.

    The power went off, or PraiseAV closed

    Open PraiseAV again. Your current service is still there: it’s kept as you work, all the time.

    A song or service is missing

    • Saved services: click Open. Check Where services are kept: are they in OneDrive on the other computer?
    • Songs: search in the Songs tab by a line of the words, not just the title.
    • Bring them back from a backup: Open › Backup and moving… › Choose a backup…. Nothing you have is deleted. See Backups.

    Still stuck?

    Ask through praiseav.com. Say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened. A photo of the screen helps.
